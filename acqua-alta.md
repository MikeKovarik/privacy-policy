# Acqua Alta — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

This policy covers the Acqua Alta app for iOS and Android ("the app"). The app is made by
Michal Kovařík, an individual developer, who is the data controller under the GDPR.

Contact: kenr.mk@gmail.com

## Summary

- There are no accounts and no sign-up.
- I don't run a server for the app and receive no data about you.
- There is no analytics or crash-reporting software in the app.
- The app shows non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. Google receives some device and usage
  data to serve them (details below).

## Data stored on your device

- Your settings: theme, units, language and date format.
- A cached copy of the latest tide, forecast and weather data, so the app still works offline.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Location

When you tap the locate button, the app asks permission to use your location while the app is
open. Your location is used only to show your position on the flood map. It is not stored, not
sent to me, and not shared with Google or anyone else. The app never uses your location in the
background. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings.

## Servers the app contacts

To show live data, the app downloads public files directly from:

- **Città di Venezia** (dati.venezia.it, www.comune.venezia.it): tide levels, forecasts and
  webcam images
- **images.weserv.nl**: a public image cache, used only when the comune's server refuses a
  webcam image
- **MET Norway** (api.met.no): weather forecasts

Like any internet request, these send your IP address and basic technical details (such as your
operating system) to those servers. The app sends them nothing else about you. Their own privacy
policies apply.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC (and by Google Ireland Limited in the
EEA and Switzerland). The app requests **non-personalised ads only**. These are chosen from
context, such as the app itself and your approximate location, not from a profile of you.
The app does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

To serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud,
Google's software in the app collects:

- your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
- device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version and language
- device identifiers: on iOS, an identifier limited to this developer's apps (IDFV); on Android,
  the advertising ID and app set ID. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your
  device's privacy settings.
- your interactions with ads, such as views and taps
- diagnostic data about how the ads load and perform

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in funding a free app through non-personalised ads
(GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Google processes this data as described at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in its privacy policy at
https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## How long data is kept

I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to keep or delete. Data on your device is
kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google keeps ad data according to its
retention policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port
your personal data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your national data
protection authority. I hold no personal data about you. For data Google collects through ads,
use Google's privacy tools: https://policies.google.com/privacy#infochoices. You can stop all
data collection by the app by uninstalling it. For any question, contact me at the address above.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in countries where that is the age of
digital consent.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
